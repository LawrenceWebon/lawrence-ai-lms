# Product Decisions

Status: **active**

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-001 | PDF-to-structured-course is the core MVP journey. | This is the defining product value requested by the project owner. | Ingestion and course generation move ahead of unrelated LMS breadth. |
| P-002 | Generated content is always a draft until a human publishes it. | Educational quality and source interpretation require accountable review. | AI and worker identities cannot publish. |
| P-003 | Use a private multi-tenant LMS foundation. | Tenant ownership and authorization are required without building a public marketplace. | Minimum auth, membership, roles, and tenant isolation stay in scope. |
| P-004 | Keep commerce, AI chat/RAG, and advanced LMS features outside the focused MVP. | They do not contribute to the defining journey and would delay delivery. | No routes, credentials, providers, schema, or UI for those capabilities. |
| P-005 | Use synthetic or rights-cleared local data during implementation. | Privacy, retention, capacity, recovery, and provider approvals are not production-ready. | Real customer data and production enablement remain blocked. |
| P-006 | `docs/product/spec.md` is the concise product contract; `docs/plan` holds detailed implementation constraints. | A small entry point prevents agents from loading or recreating the entire plan for every task. | Scope changes update the spec, features, affected plan sections, and decisions together. |
| P-007 | Use up to four independent issue/branch/worktree/PR lanes. | Parallel work should not create file, resource, migration, or branch collisions. | Contracts freeze first; shared hotspots have one integration owner. |
| P-008 | Store repository skills in `.agents/skills`; reserve `.codex/config.toml` for project settings. | This follows the supported Codex repository customization layout. | Skills remain discoverable without duplicating them under `.codex`. |
| P-009 | The project-owner PDF-to-course instruction supersedes the older post-MVP disposition only for PDF admission, extraction/OCR, structured draft generation, human review/publication, and learner consumption. | The older audit baseline predates the narrowed product focus. | These capabilities may be planned and implemented locally with synthetic or rights-cleared fixtures; external providers, real data, and production activation still require their safety and operational gates. AI companion/RAG, vector indexing unless proven necessary, commerce, and unrelated LMS breadth remain deferred. |
| P-010 | The first pilot permits an authorized instructor to review and publish their own generated draft unless tenant policy requires a separate qualified reviewer. | The defining owner-approved journey assigns review, editing, and publication to the instructor while preserving stricter review for higher-risk cases. | Human publication is always required; high-risk rights/content exceptions require the qualified separate review or audited override defined in the detailed plan. |
| P-011 | PDF is the only required source format and generated assessments are outside the focused MVP. | Additional import formats and assessment workflows are not needed to validate the defining product journey. | EPUB, DOCX, HTML, page-image import, quizzes, assignments, and assessment generation remain deferred until separately approved. |
| P-012 | The first canonical course vertical slice supports one structured rich-text block with paragraphs, headings, lists, text, and a minimal mark allowlist. | This is the smallest provider-neutral shape that produces a readable course, supports later generated drafts, and avoids committing arbitrary HTML, media, links, assets, or an editor framework. | Q-P04 is closed for F-002/F-005 contract work. Unknown nodes/marks and raw HTML/embed/provider payloads are rejected; broader block types require a separate product decision. |
| P-013 | F-003 uses a bounded local-only PDF-admission envelope: PDF signature/MIME only; 6 MiB/file; 100 pages; 25 million rendered pixels/page and 250 million/source; 64 MiB decoded parser material; 15 CPU seconds/30 wall seconds; 15-minute upload intents; two active uploads, ten upload intents and 30 MiB upload-attempt bytes per tenant rolling 24 hours; and 20 quarantined objects/60 MiB per tenant. | The project owner directed F-003 planning to set initial local limits before implementation. The envelope is deliberately sized only for synthetic or rights-cleared fixtures and makes local admission fail closed instead of implying production capacity. | Q-P03 is closed only for local F-003 admission. Production, provider, real-data, retention, recovery, capacity, and regional enablement remain separately blocked by Q-P06/Q-P07 and documents 25–28. TUS and any larger upload class require a later approved storage/provider decision. |
